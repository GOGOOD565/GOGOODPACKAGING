✦ Our Story

Born in Shanghai.
Built for the World.

GoGoodPackaging was established with a single conviction: that great packaging should be accessible to every brand, anywhere in the world — without compromising on quality, speed, or service.

We operate our own production facilities in Shanghai and Wenzhou, giving us a vertically integrated supply chain from raw material selection all the way through printing, finishing, and global dispatch. This means no guesswork, no hidden subcontracting — just direct accountability at every step.

Over 14 years later, we serve 500+ clients across 50+ countries — from independent coffee brands in the Middle East to retail chains in Europe and South America. Every order, large or small, receives the same attention to craft and detail.
